# Ad formats

## Formats (15 s unless noted)

| Format | Beats | Visual plan |
|---|---|---|
| Problem → solution | pain (3 s) → product intro (3 s) → how it fixes it (6 s) → CTA (3 s) | frustrated moment → product hero → product in use → end card |
| UGC testimonial | hook line to camera → personal story → result → CTA | presenter selfie-style, handheld, natural light |
| Unboxing | anticipation → reveal → details → reaction → CTA | top-down hands, macro details |
| Before / after | before state → transition → after state → CTA | matched framing, whip or wipe transition |
| 3 reasons | hook → reason 1/2/3 (3 s each) → CTA | one visual per reason, numbered captions |
| Demo (30 s) | hook → step 1/2/3 → result → CTA | screen or hands, clear progression |

## Hook bank (adapt, keep truthful)

- "Stop doing <common mistake> — do this instead."
- "I didn't believe <claim> until I tried it."
- "<Audience>, this is for you."
- "The <product category> nobody talks about."
- "3 reasons I switched to <product>."
- "POV: you finally fixed <problem>."
- Visual hook: the most surprising frame of the ad, shown first.

## Timing

- 0–1 s: motion + bold caption. 1–3 s: product visible. Last 2 s: CTA + brand.
- Cut every 1.5–3 s on 9:16.
- Words: 15 s ≈ 35–40, 30 s ≈ 70–80.

## Safe zones (9:16, 1080×1920)

Keep text and product out of the top ~230 px and bottom ~380 px, and the right ~120 px
(platform buttons).
