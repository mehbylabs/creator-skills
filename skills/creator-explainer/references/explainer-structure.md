# Explainer structure

## Shape

1. **Hook (0–5 s)** — a question, surprising fact, or stakes. No greetings, no "in this video".
2. **Context (1 beat)** — why it matters to *this* audience.
3. **Core beats (2–5)** — one idea each, concrete example per beat, plain words.
4. **Payoff** — the takeaway in one sentence.
5. **CTA (optional)** — one action, ≤ 1 sentence.

## Pacing

| Length | Words | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 30 s (Short) | 70–80 | 4–6 |
| 60 s | 140–160 | 6–9 |
| 90 s | 210–240 | 8–12 |
| 3 min | 420–480 | 15–20 |

- Change the visual every 3–6 s on vertical, 5–10 s on 16:9.
- One sentence ≈ one visual idea. If a sentence needs two visuals, split it.

## Writing for voice

- Short sentences; active voice; second person ("you").
- Numbers: round and contextualize ("about one in four", not "24.7%") unless precision is the point.
- Avoid parentheses, lists read aloud, and URLs.

## Accuracy

- Every factual claim must come from the user's source or be common knowledge; flag
  anything uncertain in the script for the user to verify before voicing.
- Don't invent quotes, statistics, or studies.
