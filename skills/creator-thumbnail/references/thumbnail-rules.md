# Thumbnail rules

## Composition

- **One focal point** at ≥ 40% of the frame. Faces: eyes near the top third line.
- **Three elements max**: subject, one supporting object/context, optional headline.
- **Rule of thirds**: subject on one third, headline on the opposite third.
- **Depth**: subject sharp and bright, background darker/blurred → separation at small size.
- Keep the **bottom-right** clear (timestamp overlay) and avoid the outer 5% edge.

## Color and contrast

- Complementary pairs read fastest: orange/teal, yellow/purple, red/cyan.
- Subject must contrast with background in both hue and brightness.
- Avoid YouTube's own UI colors dominating (pure white/red backgrounds vanish in the feed).

## Text

- 0–4 words, ≥ 12% of image height, heavy sans, white or yellow with dark stroke or box.
- Text complements the title ("I WAS WRONG" + title "Why I sold my Tesla"), never repeats it.
- Generate art without text; overlay type locally for crisp, correctly spelled words.

## Emotion and honesty

- Real, readable emotion beats exaggerated shock faces; match the video's tone.
- The thumbnail must reflect something that actually happens in the video.

## Variants worth testing

| Axis | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Emotion | curious | triumphant |
| Framing | close-up face | face + object |
| Color | warm | cool |
| Text | headline | no text |

## Prompt skeleton

`High-impact YouTube thumbnail photo, [subject + emotion] on the [right] third, [one
supporting object], [background] darker and slightly blurred, rim light separating subject,
saturated [color pair] palette, generous empty space on the [left] third for a headline,
no text, no logos, no watermark.`
